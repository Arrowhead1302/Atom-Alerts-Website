FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

What is Atom Alerts, exactly?

It's a Discord server. When something you collect restocks at retail price, your phone gets a notification with the store, the price and a link. You tap the link and check out on the store's own website like you normally would.

I've never used Discord. Is it hard?

Not at all. Joining takes about two minutes, we walk you through every step with pictures, and if you get stuck you can ask in #ask-for-help.

Will I definitely get the item?

Nobody can promise that; popular items still sell out. Atom Alerts gives you a fair shot by telling you the moment something is back at retail price.

Is this for resellers?

No. Atom Alerts is built for collectors and parents who want to pay retail price, not flip items for profit.

Which stores do you watch?

Target, Walmart, Amazon, Pokémon Center, Staples, American Eagle and more, plus Walmart's drawings. We add stores as members ask for them.

Can I get only the alerts I care about?

Yes. You pick your pings, like just NeeDoh, or just Pokémon at Target, and you'll only see those channels.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, in your Whop account. If you cancel during the 5-day trial, you won't be charged.
