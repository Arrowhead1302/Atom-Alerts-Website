Post a photo of an item you bought in the #wins channel of the Atom Alerts Discord server during the Entry Period. Every member of the server can post in #wins. One entry per person, no matter how many wins you post.

  • The photo must be your own picture of something you purchased (for example the item, or the item with your receipt or order confirmation). Screenshots of someone else's post, stock photos and memes don't count.
  • #wins only accepts photos. Messages without a photo, such as "enter me" or "giveaway," are removed automatically and don't count. Text is fine when it comes with a photo.
  • Sponsor may remove posts that don't follow these rules, and removed posts don't count. Entries are counted from the posts still in #wins when the winners are drawn.
