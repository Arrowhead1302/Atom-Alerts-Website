The giveaway is run by Atom Alerts ("Sponsor"). Contact: cj@atom-alerts.com.

This promotion has two parts with separate prizes: the Main Giveaway, open to anyone who meets the rules above, and the #wins Giveaway, for members of the Atom Alerts Discord server. Each prize goes to a different person; no one can win more than one prize across both giveaways.

5. How to enter the Main Giveaway

To enter, complete all of these during the Entry Period:

  1. Follow @atomalerts on Instagram and Atom Alerts on Facebook.
  2. Comment on the official Atom Alerts giveaway post: on Instagram, tag two friends in your comment; or on Facebook, leave a comment (tagging isn't required on Facebook).
  3. Join the Atom Alerts Discord server using the invite link on this page (a free Discord account is required; joining the server costs nothing and doesn't require a paid membership), open the #giveaway channel, tap Enter and give your Instagram username and Facebook name.

Every member of the server can enter, whether or not they have a paid membership. One entry per person, no matter how many comments you leave. Using more than one Discord account, fake accounts, bots or scripts disqualifies all of that person's entries. Each winner's follows and comment are checked before the prize is awarded; a winner who didn't complete every step is disqualified and a new winner is drawn.

6. How to enter the #wins Giveaway

Post a photo of an item you bought in the #wins channel of the Atom Alerts Discord server during the Entry Period. Every member of the server can post in #wins. One entry per person, no matter how many wins you post.

  • The photo must be your own picture of something you purchased (for example the item, or the item with your receipt or order confirmation). Screenshots of someone else's post, stock photos and memes don't count.
  • #wins only accepts photos. Messages without a photo, such as "enter me" or "giveaway," are removed automatically and don't count. Text is fine when it comes with a photo.
  • Sponsor may remove posts that don't follow these rules, and removed posts don't count. Entries are counted from the posts still in #wins when the winners are drawn.
