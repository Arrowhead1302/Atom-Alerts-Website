Why these sell out so fast

Popular NeeDohs and new Pokémon TCG sets don't sit on Amazon for long. When Amazon itself restocks an item at its normal price, it's often gone in minutes: resale bots and people watching for it buy it up, and the listing falls back to third-party sellers asking two or three times the price.

That's the problem Atom Alerts is built for. We watch these items around the clock and ping you on Discord the second Amazon (or Target, Walmart, Pokémon Center and other stores) has them at retail price, so parents and collectors get a fair shot instead of paying a reseller.

How to buy a restock at retail price

1. Check who's selling it

On the Amazon page, look at the buy box for "Ships from Amazon, Sold by Amazon". If it names a different seller, it's usually a reseller's price, even when the page looks the same. The restocks on this list were all sold by Amazon when they were posted.

2. Know the real price

Before you buy, compare the price with what Target, Walmart or the brand's own site charge for the same item. If Amazon's price is well above that, it's probably a third-party seller, and it's worth waiting for the next restock.

3. Be ready before it drops

Stay signed in to Amazon on your phone with a saved address and payment method. When a restock hits, add it to your cart and check out right away: an item in your cart isn't held for you, and the stock usually goes in a few minutes.

4. Don't panic-buy

Popular items restock again and again; you'll often see the same name on this list more than once. Missing one isn't a reason to pay double.
